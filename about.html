About FoodBridge

FoodBridge is a community-focused website designed to make finding food support easier.

Our goal is to connect people with food banks, community meals, volunteers, resturaunts, and organizations that can provide support.

Our Mission

Our mission is to help build a stronger community where people can more easily find food resources and oppurtunities to help others.

Who FoodBridge Supports

  • Individuals looking for food assistance
  • Families needing community support
  • Local food organizations
  • Resturaunts interested in donating food
  • Community volunteers

Get Involved

Everyone has something they can contribute. You can donate, volunteer, or simply help someone find the resources they need.
